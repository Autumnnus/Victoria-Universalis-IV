# Victoria Universalis IV

> Give every nation a doctrine, an identity, and a long-term path through the Victorian century.

**Victoria Universalis IV** is a Victoria 3 overhaul mod that brings the strategic character of Europa Universalis-style national development into Victoria 3. Build a country around enduring ideas, manage the relationship between state and society, pursue an identity for your nation, and turn your achievements into momentum for the next age.

[Subscribe on Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2946470694)

## Shape the country you want to become

Victoria 3 is a game of industrialisation, reform, and political pressure. Victoria Universalis IV adds a layer of national direction over those systems. Your laws, economy, population, institutions, and military development do not simply produce numbers: they open paths, create trade-offs, and define the kind of state you are building.

Choose doctrines that suit your plans. Build a confessional state, a civic nation, a military-industrial power, a trading empire, or a reforming society. The choices are designed to interact with Victoria 3's existing systems rather than replace them.

## What the mod adds

### National Ideas and Idea Groups

Develop your country through 21 idea groups across production, society, and military themes. Individual ideas unlock gradually through National Insight, while idea-group capacity is earned by sustained development instead of appearing automatically over time.

National Ideas give countries their own long-term character through traditions, seven progressive ideas, and a national bonus. Progress is based on the ideas you actually adopt, rewarding a focused national programme rather than a single finished tree.

### Religion with consequences

Religion is tied to your Church and State law. Each religious model has its own strengths, pressures, and relationship with the population:

- Confessional State
- Freedom of Conscience
- Total Separation
- State Atheism

Maintain Religious Settlement, spend Piety Reform on permanent programmes, and launch state-level religious projects when your government has the capacity to pursue them. Changing the religious order is a serious decision, with transition crises, Journal Entry journeys, and political consequences.

### National Identity and cultural projects

Define how your state understands the nation: as an Ethnic Nation, Civic Nation, or Composite State. Each model changes the pressures and opportunities around assimilation, migration, education, minorities, authority, and cohesion.

Earn Cultural Mandate, keep National Cohesion stable, and direct countrywide or state-targeted projects to shape the social foundations of the nation you want to build.

### Ages, momentum, and ambition

The campaign is divided into three Ages, each with objectives, rewards, and strategic opportunities. Completing objectives generates Era Momentum, which can be invested in age-specific rewards. Countries that meet the right conditions can also claim one Golden Age for a decisive period of national strength.

### Events that respond to your direction

Doctrine Journeys, Era Crossroads, national flavour chains, and project incidents give your choices a narrative dimension. These events arise from the systems you have developed: your ideas, laws, projects, country, and current age all matter.

## Interface and playability

The mod provides dedicated doctrine panels, topbar indicators, alerts for available actions, progress tracking through Journal Entries, and a read-only national doctrine overview in the country panel. The goal is to make the new systems visible and usable without losing Victoria 3's own visual language.

## Compatibility

Victoria Universalis IV changes and extends parts of the Victoria 3 interface. Interface mods that override the same GUI files may require a compatibility patch. If you use several UI mods, place Victoria Universalis IV lower in the playset so its interface changes take priority.

Major updates can change saved-game data. Starting a new campaign is recommended after a major version update; version 2.0 is not compatible with 1.x saves.
